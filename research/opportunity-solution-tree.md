# Opportunity Solution Tree

**Sources**

- **Outcome:** the North Star section of *Product Brainstorming.pdf* (pp. 4–5).
- **Opportunities:** `interview-opportunities.md`. P1 = Interview 1, P2 = Interview 2.

**How to read this**

- Every opportunity with evidence carries a word-for-word transcript quote, tagged with participant and line, e.g. (P2 L35).
- **[assumption — no quote yet]** marks opportunities with no direct quote behind them yet.
- Each opportunity is tagged with the North Star input it feeds:
  - **[ENG]** Engagement
  - **[PERS]** Level of interaction personalization
  - **[CONT]** Continuity of the shared experience
- Solution ideas marked *(brief)* come straight from the brainstorm doc's Opportunities/Interventions. The rest are new ideas that fit the Product Vision: Alternate Universe (AU) format, AI as Game Master (GM), players stay in control.

## Gaps to validate next

- **Personalization** has the least direct evidence. Most quotes about learning something new about a friend come from long, intense contexts: a week-long trip, a date. It's worth probing whether a 15-minute session can produce that feeling.
- **Remote/online play (Opportunity 13)** is in tension with the brief's "enhancing in person relationships" framing. P2 values in-person time most, and P2 L79 says in-person contact is what "reconnects that bond".
- Neither participant uses games to connect with friends (P2 L59: "Games? No"). That supports framing the product as a social activity rather than "a game".
